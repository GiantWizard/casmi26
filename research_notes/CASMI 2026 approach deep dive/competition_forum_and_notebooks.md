# Enveda CASMI 2026: competition forum, public notebooks and datasets (as of 2026-10-11)

Scope and method: every thread in the competition forum (5 list pages, roughly 95 topics, many of them team-up posts) was listed; about 60 substantive threads were read in full through Kaggle's discussion endpoint in the built-in browser, and 17 top/lineage notebooks plus 29 public dataset cards were pulled earlier with the Kaggle CLI into the scratchpad. Read-only; nothing was posted, voted, forked or submitted. Abbreviations: D = `https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/enveda-CASMI26-molecule-id-mass-spectra/discussion/`, so "D745841" means that thread. Notebooks are `https://www.kaggle.com/code/<owner>/<slug>`, datasets `https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/<owner>/<slug>`. "LB" = public leaderboard, which by participant estimates is about 130 to 132 molecules, so one molecule moving to rank 1 is worth about 0.0076 to 0.008.

## Q1. Which threads carry host clarifications, measurements or new resources, and what do they say?

### Takeaway
The hosts (David Healey, Marie Killian for Enveda; inversion and Ashley Oldacre for Kaggle) have answered almost every licensing question permissively: train.parquet and anything derived from it, MassSpecGym models (ICEBERG, GLACIER), DreaMS, CFM-ID 4, ChEBI/LIPID MAPS, COCONUT, PubChem (including subsets), newer GNPS/MassBank/MoNA releases, Enveda-180 Zenodo and even GNPS-NIST14-MATCHES are allowed; only NIST (and newly created NIST derivatives) and proprietary vendor/METLIN-style libraries are banned. FRIGID's CC BY-NC checkpoints and the ICEBERG PubChem atlas remain unanswered. The test set has no CCS/ion mobility, will not have its class breakdown revealed, solutions are stereo-stripped and the metric now uses RemoveStereochemistry.

## Q4. Posts on negative mode, adducts, formula inference, class-3 generation, rankers, ensembling, timsTOF calibration and multi-spectrum pooling

### Takeaway
Negative-mode and non-[M+H]+/[M+Na]+ molecules get no forward-model evidence in the public stack and are handled only by fragment-explanation fallbacks or ICEBERG-where-GLACIER-is-blind. Adduct relabelling is unnecessary for Enveda's own labels. Formula is almost always right; the remaining error is same-formula isomer ranking. Class-3 generation has been net negative on LB for everyone who reported it. timsTOF calibration has a now-fixed 0.5 mDa positive-mode offset. Multi-spectrum pooling matters because the test has about 3 spectra per molecule versus 4 to 5 in CV.

### Cited Findings
